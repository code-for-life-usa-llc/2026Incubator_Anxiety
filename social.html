Treatment For Social Anxiety

Available Treatments Social Anxiety

  • Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors Commonly prescribed to help increase serotonin levels in the brain, which can alleviate anxiety symptoms.
  • Exposure Therapy: Gradually exposing individuals to social situations to reduce anxiety over time.
  • Group Therapy: Practicing social skills and exposure techniques in a safe, supportive group setting with others who share similar challenges.
  • Mindfulness and Grounding:Exercises like deep breathing box breathing technique can help calm the nervous system during intense anxiety spikes.

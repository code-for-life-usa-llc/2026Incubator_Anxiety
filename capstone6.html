Our mission

Our mission is straighforward and simple: to keep you informed about social anxiety and its impact.

What is social anxiety?

Social anxiety is a mental health condition characterized by an intense fear of social situations and the potential for negative evaluation or judgment by others. It can lead to avoidance of social interactions, difficulty in forming relationships, and significant distress in various aspects of life.

How does it develop?

Social anxiety can develop due to a combination of genetic, environmental, and psychological factors. It often begins in childhood or adolescence and can persist into adulthood if left untreated.

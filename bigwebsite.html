Impact on Daily Life

If one suffers from Social Anxiety, their daily life is also affected

They experience abnormalities such as...

    Job Retention

  • Fear of job interviews or mandatory meetings can make it difficult to hold down a job or network for advancement

    Missed Oppertunities

  • Avoiding group projects, presentations, or speaking up in class can stall career growth and lower academic performance

    • Avoidance Behaviors

  • Over reliance on text; using asynchronous messaging as a shield to escape real-time social pressure

    • Isolation

  • Constantly declining invitations and skipping social gatherings leads to loneliness and smaller support networks

    • Trouble ith Intimicy

  • Fear of rejection or showing physical symptoms (like blushing or shaking) stops people from forming close bonds or romantic partnerships

All of these which can be categoriezed as 'Avoidance Behaviors'

If one were to try and place Social Anxiety on an emotional health continuum that runs from negative to positive, it would most certanly land on the left end

Student

Academic Impacts

    Not Asking for Help

  • Fear of talking to teachers stops students from getting academic support when confused

    • Lower Grades

  • Over reliance on text; using asynchronous messaging as a shield to escape real-time social pressure

Social & Emotional Well-being

    Isolation

  • Fear of talking to teachers stops students from getting academic support when confused

    • Dropping Out

  • In severe cases, the constant stress causes students to withdraw or drop out of school entirely

Young Adult

Work

    Job Limits

  • Avoiding coworkers and interviews stops young adults from getting new jobs or promotions

    • Quiting Early

  • High stress causes some to skip or quit their jobs

Friends & Family

    No practice

  • Missing social events stops them from learning normal communication skills

    • Low self-esteem

  • Constant self-criticism makes young adults feel bad about themselves

Adult

Daily Live & Work

    Job Limits

  • Adults may skip promotions, avoid public speaking, or turn down jobs that require teamwork

    • Task avoidance

  • Simple errands like making phone calls, eating in public, or asking for help in a store feel overwhelming

Relationships

    Dating fears

  • Fear of rejection makes finding or keeping a romantic partner very difficult

    • Trust issues

  • Adults may assume others are critical or unkind, which strains family and social bonds

Elderly

Diognosis & Treatment Barriers

    Silent Suffering

  • Many seniors grew up with a strong stigma against mental health struggles, leading them to hide their fears or assume feeling nervous is normal

    • Overlooked Signs

  • Doctors sometimes miss social anxiety in older patients because it gets hidden behind physical ailments or memory loss

Mental & Emotional Impact

    Intense Loneliness

  • Cutting off regular contact shrinks social networks, feeding a cycle of profound loneliness linked to depression and cognitive decline

    • Triggered Traditions

  • Retirement, the loss of a spouse, or reduced mobility can amplify the internal pressure of interacting with others
